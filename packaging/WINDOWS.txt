Plan Marks for Windows

1. Unzip this whole folder. Keep PlanMarks.exe and the _internal folder together.
2. Double-click PlanMarks.exe.
3. Your browser opens the plan marker. Leave the Plan Marks window open while you work.
4. Close that window to quit.

Windows may say it protected your PC, because this program is not signed.
Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Your PDFs stay on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.
