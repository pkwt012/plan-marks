How to mark a plan

  • Click a symbol, then click the sheet to place it. Click the symbol again to put the tool away.
  • Drag a mark to move it. Drag the amber handle to rotate the whole symbol. Hold Shift to snap to 15°.
  • Letters turn with the symbol. Drag the dividers to resize the symbol list and the tools.
  • Click a category name to show or hide its symbols.
  • Note places text on the sheet. Click where the note should sit, then type it. Drag the note off to the side.
  • Arrow points at the work. Click the first point, then click where the arrow points. Esc cancels that arrow.
  • Dashed line connects lights. Click the first point, then the next. Esc cancels the segment.
  • Drop legend places a key of every symbol on this sheet. Drag it where the crew can read it.
  • Legend in the header adds a sheet with every symbol, its name, and a short description.
  • Calibrate on a known dimension (the sample has a 10 ft line) so size can be read in feet.
  • Export PDF draws the symbols on top of the original sheet. The plan stays vector.

Shortcuts

  • Wheel zoom
  • Right-drag or Space-drag pan
  • R / Shift+R rotate 15°
  • Delete remove the selected mark
  • Ctrl+D duplicate · Alt-drag also duplicates
  • Arrows nudge · Shift nudges farther
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo
  • Ctrl+S save marks · Ctrl+O open PDF
  • Esc cancel calibrate, tool, or selection
